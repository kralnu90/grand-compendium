How to Play

👋 Lumen the helper-bot guides you through a pretend project: a small website for a pet shelter. The dark box is a pretend terminal, so nothing you do here can break anything.

Each step is one of these

  • 📖 Read: Lumen explains something. Tap Next.
  • 👆 Choose: pick the best answer. Wrong picks explain why, then you try again.
  • ⌨️ Type: type a command at the prompt. Stuck? Tap Hint.
  • 🎹 Keys: pick which keyboard key does the job.

Stars

⭐⭐⭐ for no mistakes, ⭐⭐ for one or two, ⭐ for finishing. You can replay any lesson to improve.

Handy

  • Tap the terminal to skip its typing animation.
  • ⏸ pauses. Your place is saved automatically.
  • The Cheat sheet on the Lessons screen lists everything you learn.
